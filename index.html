Welcome to Nootan Public School

“Education is not just preparation for life; education is life itself.”

At Nootan Public School (NPS), we believe every child is born with unique potential and boundless curiosity. Established with a commitment to educational excellence and grounded in strong moral values, our school provides a safe, vibrant, and nurturing environment for students from Play (Pre-Nursery) through Class 8.

We don't just teach from textbooks — we foster critical thinking, creativity, emotional intelligence, and mutual respect. Together with our parents and educators, we build a community where every milestone is celebrated and every student thrives.
