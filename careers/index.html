Careers at Nootan Public School

Join a passionate community of educators and mentors dedicated to shaping young minds. At Nootan Public School, we believe that inspired teachers inspire students.

We provide a supportive, collaborative, and growth-oriented environment where your teaching craft is valued and encouraged.

Why Teach at NPS?

  • Collaborative CultureWork alongside caring educators who share ideas, lesson plans, and teaching strategies.
  • Professional GrowthRegular workshops, pedagogical training, and opportunities to lead student initiatives and Olympiad mentoring.
  • Modern ResourcesAccess to digital learning tools, computer labs, and activity-based teaching aids.
  • Supportive CommunityA warm, respectful atmosphere where staff well-being and contributions are celebrated.

Application Process

  1. Submit your application: Send an updated résumé or CV with a brief cover letter highlighting your teaching philosophy, or complete the form below.
  2. Initial interaction & discussion: Shortlisted candidates are invited for a personal or virtual interview to discuss subject expertise and pedagogical approach.
  3. Demo class & final onboarding: Conduct a short demo classroom session with students or a panel, followed by formal appointment and orientation.
