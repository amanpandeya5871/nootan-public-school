Classes and a continuous path

The school teaches Seedling through Class 8 as one sequence. Seedling and Sapling introduce language, number, and classroom routine. Advanced Sapling adds computer as a subject. Classes 1 to 5 keep English, Hindi, mathematics, environmental studies, art, and computer. Classes 6 to 8 widen the map: science and social studies in place of EVS, Sanskrit beside Hindi, and computer continuing through the timetable.

Marks and class rank are recorded for the office and for parents. Look up a child’s marksheet on the Results page.

  • SeedlingEnglish, Hindi, Maths, EVS, Art
  • SaplingEnglish, Hindi, Maths, EVS, Art
  • Advanced SaplingEnglish, Hindi, Maths, EVS, Computer
  • Class 1–5English, Hindi, Maths, EVS, Art, Computer
  • Class 6–8English, Hindi, Maths, Science, Social Studies, Sanskrit, Computer

Examinations

The year is examined in five stages: 1st Term, 2nd Term, Half Yearly, 3rd Term, and Annual. Dates for each paper are issued as circulars.

Academic calendar 2026–27

The session shown on this site is 2026–27. Holidays, parent–teacher meetings, and assembly days are issued by the office. Open the Notices for current circulars, and treat a message from the office as the calendar that counts.

Language and method

Classroom work uses Hindi and English. Circulars may arrive in either language; if a notice is unclear, write to npsd1970@gmail.com. Homework is meant to be finishable. Questions about extra help for a particular child belong with the class teacher or the office.
