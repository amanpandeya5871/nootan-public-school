Academic year 2026–27

Seats are considered for Seedling through Class 8. Admission is an office process: a conversation, the papers the school asks for, and the fee rules explained in person or by email. There is no online application that takes money on this website.

Age and previous class matter. The office will advise whether a child can join Seedling, a later early-years group, or a numbered class. Fee amounts are given in an official note, not as a public table.

How to enquire

Email npsd1970@gmail.com with the child’s name, age or class sought, and a phone number the office can use. You may visit the school in Dharhara — write first so someone can receive you. The contact page form opens Gmail with the school address already filled.

Papers the office usually asks for

After first contact the office will confirm the list for this year. Families are often asked for proof of the child’s age or class, and — if the child is moving from another school — a leaving record from that school. Identity documents belong at the office, on paper or by the channel the office names.

Uniform and books

Uniform pattern and the book list are issued by the office. Ask when you enquire.

Transfer or leaving certificate

A transfer or leaving certificate is issued at the office. Write or visit and ask for the steps for this year.
