Our Evaluation Framework

At NPS, academic assessment is not just about marks—it is a celebration of continuous progress, conceptual clarity, and individual potential.

  • Continuous & Comprehensive AssessmentRegular formative class checks, periodic assessments, and term-end evaluations designed to track concept mastery without exam anxiety.
  • Skill & Activity-Based ScoringAssessing practical application, oral fluency, project work, and creative problem-solving alongside written tests.
  • Regular Parent-Teacher Progress MeetsTransparent academic review sessions to discuss each child's personal milestones and learning strategies.

Student Achievements & Highlights

  • 100% Foundational TransitionConsistent milestone achievement across early childhood stages (Play, Seedling, Sapling, and Advanced Sapling) into primary grades.
  • Competitive Olympiads & ContestsActive student participation and recognition in regional talent exams, science challenges, and national Olympiad platforms.
  • Co-Curricular ExcellenceInter-school awards in debate, arts, sports, and cultural presentations.

See your results

Marks are looked up in the school results app. Guardians enter the child’s details and open the marksheet.

You will need the examination (1st Term, 2nd Term, Half Yearly, 3rd Term, or Annual), class, section, student name, and roll.
